About
EssentialBits

Hi — I'm the person behind EssentialBits.pro.

Essential Bits, or EBits, was made to be a collection of free everyday tools that simplify those small annoying tasks — merging your PDFs, compressing images, extracting audio, and a lot more. Every tool is built to do one small annoying thing quickly and then get out of your way.

The idea behind it is simple. Everything runs inside your browser, so your files never touch a server — they stay on your device and are processed locally. This website is free; you don't need an account to use it, and you'll never hit a pop-up, a countdown timer, or an ad begging for your attention. You just open a tool and use it.

I built it because most "free" online tools bury you in ads, watermarks, and upsells — or quietly upload your private files to who-knows-where. I wanted the opposite: genuinely useful tools that respect you and your privacy. This website is a work in progress, and we are always open for your ideas!

Keeping EssentialBits free, private, and pop-up-free costs real money — servers, domains, and a lot of late nights building and fixing things. If a tool here saved you time or a headache, a coffee helps keep the lights on.

Frequently asked questions
Is it really free?
Yes. Every tool here is free to use, with no account and no sign-up. Down the line there may be an optional Premium tier with extra advanced features, but the essentials will always stay free.
Are my files private? What happens to them?
Your files never leave your device. Almost every tool processes your files entirely inside your own browser — nothing is uploaded to, stored on, or shared with any server. When you close the tab, they're gone from memory. For the full details, see our Privacy Policy.
Do I need to install anything?
No — everything runs right in your web browser. No downloads, no plugins, no extensions. Just open a tool and start using it.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. The tools are built to work on phones and tablets, including iPhone and iPad, as well as desktop. A few of the heavier video tools work best on a computer, but most run fine anywhere.
Why do some tools take a moment to load?
Because the processing happens on your device rather than a server, some tools load a bit of software into your browser the first time you use them. After that first load it's quick — and it's the trade-off that keeps your files private.
How can I support the site or suggest a tool?
The best way to support EssentialBits is to buy me a coffee on Ko-fi, or simply to share it with someone who'd find it useful. Got an idea for a tool or found a bug? Email me at hello@essentialbits.pro — I read every message.
