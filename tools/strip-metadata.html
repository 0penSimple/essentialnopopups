Good to know

  • Everything happens in your browser — your images are never uploaded anywhere.
  • GPS location fields are highlighted in yellow — these are the most sensitive to remove before sharing.
  • The cleaner inspects EXIF, GPS, IPTC, XMP, ICC profiles, comments, thumbnails, C2PA Content Credentials, optional container blocks, and trailing data.
  • Images are decoded to visible pixels, re-encoded, structurally cleaned, and scanned again before download.
  • PNG files keep transparency. JPG and WebP files are re-encoded at 95% quality, so the file is not byte-for-byte unchanged.
  • This does not remove visible marks, pixel-level AI watermarks, steganography, or statistical text watermarks.
