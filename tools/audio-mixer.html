Good to know

  • Everything happens in your browser — your audio is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Use the play button to hear your changes live before downloading.
  • Speed and pitch are linked in the live preview (like a record player). Turn on high-quality mode before downloading to change one without the other — it takes a little longer to process.
  • MP3 is best for sharing and general use; WAV is lossless and larger, best for editing software.
