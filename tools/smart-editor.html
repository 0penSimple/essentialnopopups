Good to know

  • Your work is auto-saved in your browser locally every 2 seconds.
  • Click 🕒 Version History to view point-in-time snapshots or create manual restore points.
  • Toggle ✨ Audit for English style hints: possible passive voice (violet), wordiness (yellow), and repeated words (red). Click a hint to locate it. These rules are suggestions, not a complete grammar check.
  • Turn on 🎧 Sound for ambient background audio (rain, brown noise, warm room tone) and soft typing taps. Click Sound again or Stop all to silence everything.
